About Lumina Studio

Philosophy, vision, and architectural craftsmanship

We design spaces that converse with light, material, and nature.

Founded on the principle that form should elevate the human experience, Lumina Studio specializes in high-concept residential, commercial, and civic architecture. Each space begins not with geometry, but with an inquiry into the site’s geography, climate, and daily rhythms.

From monolithic concrete pavilions to minimal glass sanctuaries, our practice is rooted in honest materials, sculptural clarity, and enduring proportion.
